you made it to state. now look like it.

When your team qualifies for a state tournament, the window between "we're in!" and game day is tiny. State shirts are our rush specialty: quick turnarounds, online store capabilities, and no rush fees. Whether it's a state tournament or the Drake Relays, it's never too early to start preparing, and it has never been easier.

how it works.

reach out.

Use the quote form the moment you qualify (or before). It collects everything at once so you can turn your focus back to preparation, not the shirts.

we handle the art.

With 30 minutes of free art time, the possibilities are endless. Our in-house art department turns your school, sport, and bracket run into a design; your approval is all we need.

store goes live when you win.

If your sport's timeline allows, we build the online store ahead of time and open it the day of your qualifying competition, ready for your team, families, fans, and school community. Store windows close quickly, so having it ready helps everyone.

we produce and pack.

Once the store closes we get to work, producing everything and packaging it individually by order to make distribution easy. Pick up individually at our shop, send a representative for the whole batch, or ask about delivery options.

show up proud.

Your team, families, fans, and school community rep your colors as you take the court, pitch, field, track, pool, or mat.

Some sports have tighter windows than others between qualifying and the state tournament (looking at you, soccer), but we have processes in place to get those done too.

Every sport, every season, every level. Football, volleyball, wrestling, basketball, soccer, track, cheer, dance, esports. If they're headed to state, we can do that.

common questions.

How fast can you turn state tournament shirts?

This is our rush specialty, and state tournament orders carry no rush fees. Reach out the moment you qualify and we'll have art moving the same day; if we have the garments in stock we can turn an order same day, generally next day if we need to order them.

Do you do Drake Relays shirts?

Yes. The same quick-turnaround, no-rush-fee process covers the Drake Relays and other qualifying meets, with an online store ready to open the moment your athletes punch their ticket.

Can fans and parents order their own shirts?

Yes. We can spin up a quick online store so fans and parents pick their own sizes and pay online, with nothing for the booster club to collect, sort, or front.

Do you design the shirt for us?

Yes. Our in-house art department turns your school, sport, and bracket run into a custom design. The first 30 minutes of art time is free when your order includes production.
