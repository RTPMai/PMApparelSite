press & recognition.

Podcasts, articles, and a few nice things other people in the industry have said about us. We didn't write any of these.

recognition.

In 2024, Megan Griffith won the Women in Screen Printing Award from Screen Printing Mag. She was also part of the magazine's inaugural Rising Stars class. She sits on the Apparelist Advisory Board and writes a recurring column for GRAPHICS PRO.

Ryan Toney was a member of the Gildan Board of Decorators and founded Flyover Con, a free industry conference hosted right on our production floor. He currently serves on Chipply's Client Council and the Polk City Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors.
