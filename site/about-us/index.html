our story is driving your story.

Third-generation. Woman-owned. Family-run since 1987.

literally a mom and pop shop.

P&M Apparel started in the basement of Phyllis and Melvin (the P and the M), who took up custom apparel as a new career after farming and remodeling homes most of their lives. Their daughter Kay took over a few years after the business moved to Polk City, and two of her children, Megan and Ryan, joined the ranks as it grew. Megan and Ryan took over ownership in 2023.

Since 1987, we've helped businesses, schools, nonprofits, and communities create apparel people are proud to wear, through screen printing, embroidery, promotional products, online stores, and on-demand fulfillment. We've grown from Central Iowa to the whole state, the country, and lately, the world. Last year alone we shipped to all 50 states and 29 countries.

what we believe.

Great apparel builds belonging. Great service still matters. Fast answers matter. Keeping your word matters. That mindset has carried P&M through decades of change while we keep evolving with better systems, stronger creativity, smarter technology, and a team that genuinely cares.

We're not just decorators. We're problem solvers, idea generators, deadline hitters, and brand builders. We combine real production knowledge with practical business thinking, so you get solutions that work in the real world, not just on paper.

90% of our business comes from referrals. That's not an accident. It's the relationships.

We've also had a few people outside our shop take notice. See what's been said in press & recognition.

the photo on the press.

Walk the production floor and you'll find a small snapshot taped to the side of one of our presses: three generations of the women who built this company. It's not decoration. It's a reminder of whose name is on the sign.
