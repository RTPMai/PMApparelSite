giving back.

Scholarships, sponsorships, and donations for the communities that have supported us for nearly 40 years.

shirts for scholarships.

The P&M Apparel Shirts for Scholarships Fund is dedicated to helping youth in the North Polk, Ankeny, Woodward-Granger, and Saydel community school districts attend and succeed in the educational arena. Its vision: empowering Central Iowa youth with need-based educational resources.

Each year we award need-based scholarships to deserving graduating high school seniors pursuing a course of study at a two-year or four-year college, university, or trade program. Recipients are selected on need and program criteria, and funds are awarded after proof of enrollment.

For 2026: the fund's award ceiling is $2,000, with an individual maximum award of $1,000. Applications are due before May 22 at 5:00 pm. Download the application.

sponsorships and donations.

Teams, events, fundraisers, and organizations can submit a sponsorship request any time. We review every request and support as many as we can. We're always grateful to support the people who support our community.
